What is the Openbtk AI ecosystem?

Openbtk AI is an open source project, not a product lineup — a growing collection of tools for biomedical AI that share one goal: help healthcare enterprises, developers, and researchers build next-generation GenAI applications without re-solving the same infrastructure problems. Openbtk is where it starts.

What that actually solves, in a traditional biomedical AI or agent build:

🔒

Build AI your compliance team approves

Patient data is de-identified before it ever reaches a model — so your team can build clinical chatbots and assistants without becoming PHI-handling experts first.

📋

Answer "prove it" before it's asked

Every AI pipeline leaves a verifiable record of what happened — the audit trail enterprise healthcare organizations, auditors, and regulators expect, without extra engineering work on your side.

🗂️

AI that reads a chart like a clinician

A patient's history, assessment, and plan stay together instead of being scrambled into generic text fragments — so your RAG system gives grounded, coherent answers.

⚡

From pilot to enterprise scale, no rewrite

Built to handle real patient-record volumes from day one, so a proof-of-concept with a few hundred notes doesn't turn into a rebuild six months later.
